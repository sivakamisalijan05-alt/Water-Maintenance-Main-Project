
# 💧 Water Maintenance System

## 📌 Project Overview

The Water Maintenance System is a web-based application designed to improve water resource management and simplify the reporting of water-related issues. It allows citizens to report problems such as water scarcity, pipeline leakage, water contamination, and irregular water supply.

The system helps water board officials manage complaints, track issue status, coordinate repair activities, and handle emergency water tanker requests.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Simplify the reporting of water-related issues.
- Help citizens communicate water problems to officials.
- Improve complaint management and tracking.
- Coordinate water pipeline repairs and maintenance.
- Handle emergency water tanker requests.
- Improve water resource management and service efficiency.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Water Issue Reporting:** Citizens can report water scarcity, pipeline leakage, contamination, and irregular water supply.
- **Complaint Management:** Officials can view and manage citizen complaints.
- **Status Tracking:** Track the progress of reported issues.
- **Repair Coordination:** Help officials organize maintenance and repair activities.
- **Emergency Water Tanker Requests:** Allow citizens to request water tankers during shortages.
- **Centralized Management:** Organize water-related complaints and maintenance information.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Citizens report water-related problems through the application.
2. Water board officials review the submitted complaints.
3. Officials coordinate the required repair or maintenance activities.
4. The complaint status is updated as work progresses.
5. Citizens can submit emergency water tanker requests when necessary.
